Atlas of Wanderworlds

Play Atlas of Wanderworlds

The game says "The browser ran out of graphics memory" or won't start on my phone.

Close your other browser tabs, fully close and reopen the browser, then open the game again. On iPhone, check that Lockdown Mode is off (Settings > Privacy & Security) and that iOS is up to date. If you came from a Facebook or Instagram link, open the page in Safari or Chrome instead.

How do I get the newest version?

The game checks for updates by itself. On the title screen, a new version loads right away. In a world, you'll see "UPDATE AVAILABLE: back to the title to update".

Random join says "No hosts" but my friend is hosting.

You're probably on different versions. Both of you go back to the title (or reload the page) so you're on the newest version, then try again. You can also join with the room code your friend shares.

How do I zoom the camera?

Pinch with two fingers on a phone, or use the mouse wheel or trackpad. Zoom all the way in for first person.

How do I play full screen?

Tap the full-screen planet in the top-left corner of the title screen, or in the pause menu.

My saved world looks different.

The game is in beta, so worlds can change between versions. A red ! on the saved worlds page marks worlds saved by an older version.

I'm stuck in the terrain.

Open the pause menu and press Unstuck (the ! planet). After 10 seconds you're back at your spawn point. Press it again or walk away to cancel.

What are the controls?
  • Phone: the stick moves, drag anywhere else to look, the orange planet jumps, the hand planet uses things (seats, doors), and the smiley planet opens emotes.
  • Keyboard: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump, E to use, 1 to 6 for emotes, Esc to pause.
  • Controller: left stick moves, right stick looks, A jumps, X uses, Start pauses.
