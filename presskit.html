About

Hyper Arcane Mage Battle (HAMB) is a 2D platform battler inspired by ROUNDS and Noita. Up to 8 players enter a multi-round match and cast spells to win each round. Between rounds, everyone gets new spell cards and a build session, so every fight calls for a new strategy.

Spells are built from parts: a form, stats and modifiers. With 9 base spells, over 100 modifier cards and no cap on stacking, the combinations are practically endless. Players who place lower draw more cards, which keeps matches close between veterans and newcomers.

Features

  • Build your own spells from 9 base spells and over 100 modifier cards
  • A build session before every round, starting before the first fight
  • Fair standings: lower placings draw more cards
  • Win by wearing opponents down or knocking them into the pit
  • 1 to 8 players locally, online, over LAN or with Remote Play Together
